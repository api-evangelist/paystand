AI in finance is moving from answering questions to taking responsibility for work. Today, Paystand is introducing digital employees to its B2B payment network: AI agents with ongoing responsibilities, measurable outcomes, and clear boundaries set by finance. Digital employees give finance teams additional capacity they can direct, allowing them to handle growing transaction volume while moving their own attention upstream into governance, judgment, and capital strategy.
